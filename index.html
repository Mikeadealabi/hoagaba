The Rank

Entering the Inner Bar

The Senior Advocate of Nigeria is the highest distinction a Nigerian legal practitioner can receive. It is conferred by the Legal Practitioners' Privileges Committee on advocates of exceptional merit, integrity and standing at the Bar. Its holders are called Silks.

In 2026, Obafemi Agaba took his place among them.
